WHY GROUP BETTING BREAKS DOWN

THE SOCIAL BETTING DILEMMA & THE HACKATHON CHALLENGE

❌ THE BREAKDOWN IN MESSENGERS
  • 1. Vague & Disputed Rules: Bets like "Monaco GP will be under 80 minutes" spark endless arguments over whether red flags, safety car parade laps, or pit exit times count.
  • 2. The Unwilling Bookmaker: Nobody wants to manually hold counterparty risk, track multiple bets, or calculate continuous payouts by hand.
  • 3. Counterparty Default: Losing friends go silent, forget what was agreed, or contest the payoff math.
Result: 85% of casual group predictions are abandoned before final settlement.
🛡️ THE HACKATHON AI CHALLENGE
  • 1. Traceable Reasoning: Autonomous AI agents must parse voice and natural text into rigorous contract parameters, maintaining an auditable trace from prompt to execution.
  • 2. Mandatory Risk Guardrails: The agent must never gamble user funds without explicit confirmation of interpreted strategy, take-profit levels, and maximum drawdown floors.
  • 3. Deterministic Zero-Sum Ledger: AI suggests and lints; deterministic math settles. Points and CHIP in escrow are conserved with zero floating-point leaks.
Solution: MarketMate unites natural voice intake with multi-agent verification and formal zero-sum guarantees.
💡
Simple vs Complex: Whether friends are running a high-frequency F1 minute-window spread market with limit orders, or simply betting 5 CHIP on whether it will rain tomorrow, MarketMate's multi-agent pipeline adapts the rules instantly.

AGENT LIMITS, CONTROLS & MANDATORY RISK GATE

GATE 1 // STRATEGY CONFIRMED
Strategy Recitation

Bot recites the parsed strategy back to the trader in plain language: "You are betting ABOVE 80.0 min (expecting rain/delays) with 10 CHIP lot."

GATE 2 // TAKE-PROFIT BOUNDED
Mandatory Profit Target

Traders must specify an exit level (e.g. cash out when implied race time reaches 84 min or +30 CHIP equity). Prevents round-tripping gains.

GATE 3 // STOP FLOOR HARD STOP
Mandatory Drawdown Floor

Traders set their hard loss floor (e.g. exit if balance drops below 40% of stake). The bot registers a native unit stop-loss on the ladder.

★ THIRD-PARTY ARBITER PROTOCOL (FR-56/57) COUNTDOWN AUTO-LOCK

When a market cannot be verified via automated feeds (e.g. custom friend wagers), Agent 2 assigns an independent arbiter (e.g. a neutral friend or boss). The arbiter cannot place bets in that market, and an automated countdown timer locks all betting and withdrawals at 00:00:00.

💡
Universal Application: These limits apply across all market styles — whether a friend makes a complex F1 window bet or a simple 5 CHIP bet on a Mario Kart match. The bot always guards your capital.

DUAL EARLY-EXIT PROTOCOL

CONTINUOUS MARK-TO-MARKET & ANYTIME WITHDRAWALS

01 // REAL-TIME MARK-TO-MARKET LIVE UPDATES

Markets remain continuously live until the chequered flag drops. As lap times, pit stops, and weather telemetry arrive:

  • Implied winning time recalculates in real-time.
  • Every trader's unrealized equity is continuously marked to market.
  • Stop-losses trigger automatically when implied time crosses native unit thresholds.
02 // ANYTIME WITHDRAWAL ENG-03 PROTOCOL

Traders are never locked until final resolution. If your current position has positive equity (equity > 0):

  • Quick Cash Out: Withdraw 25%, 50%, or 100% of your active gains in one tap.
  • Exact CHIP Slicing: Specify an exact CHIP slice (e.g. withdraw 15 CHIP).
  • Zero-Sum Invariant: Withdrawn equity is released immediately while integer escrow remains perfectly balanced.
